Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how cristdulcey.com (the "Site"), operated by Cristian Dulcey ("we", "us", or "our"), collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Site and any related services or applications we operate (collectively, the "Services"), including integrations with third-party platforms such as TikTok.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly. If you contact us (for example, by email), we receive the information you choose to share, such as your name and email address.

Information from third-party platforms. If you connect a third-party account — for example, by signing in with TikTok through TikTok Login Kit — we receive only the basic profile information you authorize during the consent flow, such as your display name, avatar, and an identifier for your account (open ID). We only request the minimum scopes needed for the integration to work, and we never receive your password.

Usage data. The Site uses Google Analytics, which collects anonymized usage information (such as pages visited, approximate location, browser, and device type) through cookies. See "Analytics and Cookies" below.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the Services;
  • Authenticate you when you sign in through a third-party platform;
  • Display your profile information (such as your display name and avatar) within the Services;
  • Respond to your inquiries and messages;
  • Understand how visitors use the Site (aggregate analytics).

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share information:

  • With service providers that help us operate the Services (such as hosting and analytics providers), under appropriate safeguards;
  • When required by law, regulation, or valid legal process;
  • To protect the rights, safety, or property of our users or others.

4. Data from TikTok and Other Platforms

When you connect your TikTok account:

  • We access only the data covered by the scopes you explicitly approve (for example, basic profile information via Login Kit).
  • We use that data solely to provide the functionality you requested.
  • We do not use TikTok data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.
  • You can disconnect our application at any time from your TikTok account settings ("Security & permissions" > "Apps and services"), which revokes our access.
  • Upon disconnection or upon your request, we delete the data we obtained from your TikTok account within 30 days, unless retention is required by law.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide the Services or as required by law. Data obtained from third-party platforms is deleted when you revoke access or request deletion.

6. Analytics and Cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate site traffic. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymized usage data. You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser settings.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

8. Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum legal age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights — including requesting deletion of data obtained through TikTok — contact us at the email address below. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, you can contact us at christian.dulcey@gmail.com.
