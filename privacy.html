Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Overview

L&H Studios does not collect or receive your personal information through Spending List Pro. The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Your Data

Information you enter into Spending List Pro, including transactions, settings, and receipt photos, is stored locally on your device.

L&H Studios does not operate a server that receives or stores this information.

Photos and Camera

Spending List Pro may request access to your camera or photo library when you choose to take or attach a receipt photo.

Photos selected or captured through the app are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to L&H Studios.

Importing, Exporting, and Sharing

Spending List Pro allows you to import and export your data.

When you choose to export or share data, the app uses your device's standard sharing functionality. Your data is sent only to the destination or application you select.

Any third-party service you choose to share data with is governed by that service's own privacy practices.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's data practices change. Any changes will be published on this page.
